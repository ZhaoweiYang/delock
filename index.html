No. Delock runs entirely on the browser's native Web Crypto API. Encryption, hashing and encoding all happen locally in memory — your keys and plaintext are never uploaded. You can even use it fully offline.

Symmetric encryption (Delock Hybrid, AES-256-GCM, AES-256-CBC), hashing (SHA-256, SHA-512, MD5), and reversible encoders (Base64, Hex, URL). Keys for AES are derived with PBKDF2 using a random salt.

You provide a secret key or passphrase. Delock derives a 256-bit AES key from it with PBKDF2-SHA-256 and a freshly generated random salt, then encrypts with a random IV. The salt and IV are packaged with the ciphertext so decryption only needs your key.
